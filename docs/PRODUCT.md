# LARP product
Access is the flex. LARP provides aspirational physical experiences and digital creative assets, not a game or virtual world.
Audience: creators, artists, entrepreneurs and lifestyle enthusiasts. US-first physical launch in Los Angeles; global digital discovery.
Brand: midnight #101014, champagne #E7C58D, ivory #F5F3EF, graphite #39353C. Editorial, cinematic, premium and internet-native.
Physical categories: vehicles, properties, fashion, jewelry, production, boats, aviation sets and VIP experiences. Digital categories include founder, luxury, academia, artist and fictional persona assets.
All financial, credential and achievement assets must be clearly fictional. Prohibit impersonation, fraudulent documents and deceptive claims.
## Phase 1 assumptions
Inventory and indicative USD prices are illustrative. No actual vendors, availability, checkout or account services. Demo favorites persist only in the current browser session. Concierge and seller calls to action explain upcoming functionality.
## Open decisions
Validate commissions (proposed 10–15%) and concierge fees (15–20%); vendor verification, insurance, deposits, cancellations, tax, digital licenses, age requirements, Stripe Connect eligibility and seller payout geography before commerce.
